# Code of conduct

This project is a place to build software together. Everyone who takes part,
in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions, is expected to keep it one.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient. Assume the other person means well.
- Criticize code and ideas, not people. Explain your reasoning.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change; ask why if it is unclear.
- Keep discussion on the project.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory remarks about anyone's identity,
  background or experience.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome personal attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or pressure after a clear "no".

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers privately, through the contact details on
the repository owner's GitHub profile; do not open a public issue about it.
Content that breaks GitHub's own rules can also be reported with GitHub's
"Report content" link on the comment or profile. Reports are kept
confidential.

## What maintainers may do

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block
people from the project, temporarily or permanently, when this code is not
followed. They apply it to themselves as well.
